Literary Bio: For the last twentyoneish years I've been bummin around this blessed, godforsaken Earth—which has been quite a fine and dandy thing—but one day much sooner than I am apt to expect, I won't be above ground any longer. So methinks to meself, all this crap I produce deserves a place to exist untethered from the body. So here it is, all wrapped up on the goddamn internet. Anyhow, my name is Nathaniel Kenn Berg. Bit of a mouthful so just Nate will do. Sometimes I write, build wooden structures, draw astoundingly poorly, play instruments, fail to seduce the artistic muses, box like Frazier, drink cheap whiskey, and travel in search of feasts and jazzzzz music. I'm in love and so is she but we're far far apart. Right now i'm on the road, huntin for (mis)adventures and new colors. My most earnest wish is that we may oneday drink each other's wine.
